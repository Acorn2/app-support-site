How Support Works

What helps us resolve issues faster

Since Kindred is designed to be calm and lightweight, support should feel the same way: simple, direct, and easy to understand.

1. Describe The Issue

Tell us what happened

A short explanation of what you expected and what actually happened gives us the fastest starting point.

2. Add Device Context

Include version details

Share your device model, iOS version, and the app version you are using. If the issue is visual, screenshots are especially helpful.

3. We Reply By Email

Expect a human response

We usually reply within 3 to 5 business days. If we need more details, we will follow up by email.

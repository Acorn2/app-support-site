Support

Need help with Kindred?

If you run into an issue, have a question, or want to share product feedback, please contact us by email. We usually reply within 3 to 5 business days.

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version you are using
  • A short description of the issue
  • Screenshots if the problem is visual

Typical help topics

  • Notification reminders not appearing
  • Questions about Kindred Pro purchase status
  • Feedback about reminders or onboarding
  • General product suggestions

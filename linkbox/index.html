How Support Works

What helps us resolve issues faster

LinkBox is meant to feel calm, private, and lightweight. Support should feel the same way: direct, specific, and easy to act on.

1. Describe The Flow

Tell us what you were trying to do

For example: saving from Chrome, opening a link in Safari, running a check, or switching privacy mode on.

2. Add Device Context

Include version details

Share your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version. If the issue is visual, screenshots are especially helpful.

3. We Reply By Email

Expect a human response

We usually reply within 3 to 5 business days. If we need more context, we will follow up by email.

收链 / LinkBox 技术支持
